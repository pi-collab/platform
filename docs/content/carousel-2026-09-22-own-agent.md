# Carousel — "Every creator is their own agent"

**Status:** copy only, ready for design. Not designed slides.
**Source:** Guapd onboarding survey, n=105, pulled from production 22 Sep 2026.
**Format:** 8 slides, Instagram square. Also works on LinkedIn.

---

## Why this one

It is built on our own numbers, so no competitor can publish it and no
journalist can trace it to a vendor report. It also compounds: the same 105
creators told us both that they negotiate alone AND that their biggest
problem is not enough deals. One slide sets up the other.

## Do not claim

- **Not "creators in India".** 105 creators who signed up to Guapd and chose
  to answer. Self-selected, and skewed toward people already looking for a
  tool. Say "105 creators on Guapd" every time — it is both more honest and
  more interesting than a vague market claim.
- **Do not round to 10% and 70%** in the art while the caption says 9.5% and
  69.5%. Pick one precision and hold it across slide, caption and alt text.
- **Do not imply agencies are bad.** The data says most creators do not use
  one. It says nothing about whether they should.
- **Do not add a Guapd traction number** — no brand count, no deal count, no
  signup total. The survey size is the only number of ours that goes out.
- **Slide 8 describes structure, not guarantees.** Guapd tracks payment
  status; it does not hold or release money. "Gets you paid faster" is a
  claim we cannot support.

## Notes for design

- Slides 2 and 5 are the two that get screenshotted. Give them the whole
  canvas: one number, one line, nothing else.
- Slide 3 is three figures at once — the weakest in the set. If it feels
  crowded, cut "just starting out" and keep two.
- Slide 7 is the thesis. It should look different from the data slides.
- Put the sample size on slide 1 and again small on slide 8. A carousel gets
  screenshotted mid-way and the sample has to travel with the number.
