# Carousel B — "Not one brand said it was too expensive"

**Audience:** brands. The one that recruits demand.
**Status:** copy only, ready for design.
**Source:** Guapd brand onboarding survey, pulled from production 30 Sep 2026, **n=13**. Multi-select, so answers don't sum to 100%.
**Format:** 8 slides. Square for IG, but this one belongs on LinkedIn.

---

## Why this one

Every brand answer is an **information** problem. Not one is a price problem.

- Can't find the right creators — 69%
- Can't measure what came of it — 69%
- Can't tell if the numbers are real — 62%
- Can't keep the campaign together — 46%

Brands are not saying influencer marketing costs too much. They're saying they
can't *see* — who to pick, whether the audience exists, what happened
afterwards. That reframes the whole category away from price and onto proof,
which is the argument Guapd wants brands to accept.

Two outside facts push in the same direction and both are this quarter:
Meta just made usage rights and expiry dates native fields, and Passionfroot
raised $15M to build workflow-first creator tooling. The market is spending
money on the same gap these 13 brands just described.

**n=13.** Stated on the post. See the note at the bottom.

## ⚠️ n=13

Thirteen respondents. One brand is 7.7 percentage points — 69% is nine people,
and one different answer moves the headline eight points.

The caption states the number and calls it early days, which is the honest
framing and reads better than pretending otherwise. **A brand percentage must
never appear without n=13 attached**, including if a slide is re-cut for
stories. "69% of brands can't find creators" standing alone is the version that
gets the post taken apart; "69% of the 13 brands on Guapd" is a normally
reported small sample.

It also tells the market Guapd has about thirteen brands. That is the cost of
the post, and it is a deliberate trade.

## Do not claim

- **Do not say Meta verifies follower counts.** Partnership Ads is ad
  attribution. Slide 6 argues from *usage rights and expiry*, which is what was
  actually announced.
- **Slide 8 promises verified audience numbers.** True only for a creator who
  has connected Instagram, and Meta app review is still outstanding. If this
  post runs before that clears, soften to "audience numbers read from
  Instagram, not typed in" and be ready to show it.
- **Don't imply Passionfroot is a competitor we beat.** It is a funding signal
  for the category. Naming it as a rival invites a comparison nobody asked for.
- **Don't say "brands don't care about price."** They said it wasn't their
  *biggest challenge*. Those are different claims and slide 1 is worded for the
  first.
