# Carousel A — "Getting the deal is the easy part"

**Audience:** creators. The one that recruits supply.
**Status:** copy only, ready for design.
**Source:** Guapd creator onboarding survey, pulled from production 30 Sep 2026, **n=142**. Multi-select, so answers don't sum to 100%.
**Format:** 9 slides, Instagram square.

---

## Why this one

The obvious read of our own data is "creators want more deals" — 68% said so,
it's the top answer, and it's the least interesting thing in the dataset.

The interesting thing is what sits underneath it. The other three answers are
**not about getting deals at all**. They're about what happens once you have
one: getting paid, keeping track, proving what was agreed. A creator's problem
doesn't end when a brand says yes. That's the post.

And the external number lands right on top of it: ASCI reviewed 1,609
influencer ads and 97.3% needed fixing, mostly for disclosure and terms. When
30% of creators tell us they hold no record of what was agreed, those two facts
are the same fact.

## Do not claim

- **Slide 9 says 92% have no agency, not that agencies fail.** The claim is how
  few creators have one, not that having one doesn't work.
- **ASCI reviewed those ads** — a caseload reached through complaints and
  monitoring, not a random sample. Never "97.3% of influencer ads in India".
- **Don't let 97.3% read as fraud.** ASCI attributes most violations to
  disclosure and restricted-category issues. Slide 7 says "disclosure and
  unclear terms" for that reason.
- **Don't imply we've fixed the payment problem.** Guapd tracks payment status;
  it does not hold or release funds. Slide 4 states the problem and the close
  does not promise the cure.
- **Self-selected sample.** People who signed up to a deal-workflow product.
  The caption dates and sizes it.
