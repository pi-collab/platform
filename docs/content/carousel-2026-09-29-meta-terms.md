# Carousel — "Meta just made deal terms a database field"

**Status:** copy only, ready for design. Not designed slides.
**Source:** Meta Creator Marketing Hub / Instagram Live Partnership Ads,
announced 15 September 2026, live from 29 September 2026.
**Format:** 8 slides, Instagram square. Reads better on LinkedIn than on IG.

---

## Why this one

Meta has started storing the terms of a creator deal — usage permissions, and
an expiry date on them — as native fields rather than leaving them in a
conversation. That is the same argument Guapd makes, made by the largest
platform in the market, which is a much better position than making it alone.

**What it is not:** this is not Meta verifying follower counts. Partnership Ads
is ad *attribution* for sponsored content performance. The brief's do-not-claim
list says this outright and the slides below are written around it — the
argument runs through **terms**, not through audience numbers. Slide 6 is the
only one that touches audience data and it is deliberately conditional.

Weaker than the ASCI carousel as a hook: it is industry news rather than
something that happened to the reader. It earns its place because slide 3 is a
genuinely good line.

## Do not claim

Two from the brief, plus one that matters more than either.

- **Do not say Meta verifies follower counts or audience demographics.**
  Partnership Ads is ad attribution for sponsored-content performance. Every
  slide here argues from *terms*, not from audience data, for this reason.
- **Do not quote engagement-lift figures** ("30–40% higher engagement" and
  similar). That number comes from a secondary aggregator, not Meta's release.
- **ADDED, and the important one — slide 8 says "when a creator connects
  Instagram" and must keep saying it.** The snapshot pipeline is built and a
  connected creator's storefront does read from Instagram. But Meta app review
  is still outstanding, and as of mid-September not one signed-up creator had
  connected an account. "Guapd reads audience figures from Instagram" stated
  flatly is a claim about the product as it stands today that we cannot
  currently back. Conditional, or cut the line.
