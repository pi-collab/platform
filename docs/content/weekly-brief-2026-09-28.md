- **What:** Meta merged its creator discovery, messaging, and partnership-ad tools into a single "Creator Marketing Hub," rolling out new discovery filters, content-level usage permissions with expiry dates, and an expanded Creator Marketplace API that now covers Facebook and Instagram creators through one integration.

- **Why it matters to Guapd:** Meta is actively rebuilding the attribution layer for brand-creator deals — the more Meta formalizes usage rights and permissions natively, the more brands will expect that same structure (rights, permissions, delivery proof) from any deal workflow, which is Guapd's core product.

### ASCI's FY26 complaints report: 97.3% of reviewed influencer ads violated guidelines
- **What:** ASCI's Annual Complaints Report 2025-26 reviewed 1,609 influencer advertisements and found 97.3% required modification for non-compliance; 54% of violations involved legally restricted categories (chiefly offshore betting).
- **Number:** 1,609 influencer ads reviewed; 97.3% in violation; 54% linked to illegal/restricted categories.
- **Why it matters to Guapd:** This is not new this week — the report published May 28, 2026 — but it remains the most current hard regulatory data point on India disclosure compliance, and it's a direct argument for platforms that verify deal terms and disclosures rather than leaving them to a WhatsApp thread.
- **Source:** BuzzInContent, "ASCI flags 1,609 influencer violations in FY26; 54% linked to illegal categories," May 28, 2026 — https://www.buzzincontent.com/insight/asci-flags-1609-influencer-violations-in-fy26-54-linked-to-illegal-categories-11882137
